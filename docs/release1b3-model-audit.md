## Correctness constraints identified

1. **Closing-price leakage boundary.** The old runner feeds `CLOSING_BENCHMARK` references into `MarketResidual.predict` while persisting a PREGAME label. Its closing-conditioned outputs must not be described as forecasts based on prices captured before kickoff. New non-market predictors receive no market reference; closing lines/prices are used only after forecasting, as diagnostic targets and comparators. Prices remain source-attributed and non-executable.
2. **Replay cutoff and label must agree.** `sequential_ratings_moments` advances at kickoff, not `horizon_as_of(PREGAME)`. A result arriving between a declared pregame cutoff and kickoff can therefore be included by the old replay. New reporting must explicitly label its actual kickoff retrospective information clock, or implement and test a genuinely horizon-aware replay. No live earlier-vintage claim follows.
3. **Historical registry names are mutable in the legacy runner.** `register_model`, prediction persistence and calibration persistence use `session.merge` with reusable names. Running new folds in a populated historical database can replace metadata or hit immutability protections. Use a fresh attested workspace, unique run/fold versions and retained immutable reports. Preserve all old evaluations, including conflicts.
4. **Burned seasons.** `docs/model-governance.md` expressly identifies 2024 and 2025 as inspected. Chronological replays of those years are reproducibility/development evidence, not untouched prospective evidence. Newly chosen models require a frozen future cohort before stronger qualification.
5. **Calibration selection is not chronological.** Existing `select_calibration` uses seeded shuffled five-fold validation. It protects each row against in-sample fitting, but is not forward-only within a season. Identity calibration avoids implying otherwise. Future recalibration must fit earlier observations and score later observations without consulting the held-out season.
6. **Do not mistake missing features for a validated model.** A games-only load leaves `RollingAverageBaseline` without team rows, causing fallback predictions; the ridge model imputes absent play-by-play columns. Explicit score-only history is a legitimate rolling baseline, but is not a full EPA/usage feature experiment. Report the exact feature set and missingness.
7. **Timestamp approximations remain approximations.** `load_games_csv` models result availability as kickoff plus 4h30m and historical closing observations at kickoff. These are not verified source publication timestamps. Re-downloaded historical files may contain later corrections; genuine NFL outcomes do not imply historically captured vintages. Report this limitation and avoid strict prospective availability claims.
8. **Fresh ratings instances.** `DynamicRatings._reset` does not reset its learned HFA; repeatedly fitting the same object can carry information across fits. Instantiate separately for every candidate/fold. Simultaneous updates and source order must remain deterministic.
9. **Distribution limits.** Independent normal margin/total marginals miss key-number concentration, joint score constraints, tail structure and within-game dependencies. In-sample residual estimates can understate uncertainty. Report interval coverage and sensitivity rather than treating fitted scale as validated confidence.
10. **Evaluation completeness.** Existing `score_moments` excludes ties from binary win scoring and reports a separate `n_win_scored`; retain that denominator. Report spreads/totals at matched lines, pushes separately, paired same-game differences against baselines, week-block intervals, season-specific results and sensitivity. A generic 0.5 spread probability is a simple comparator, not a captured no-vig sportsbook quote.

## Execution and evidence strategy

The callable legacy path is `ingest.ingest_dataset` with `NflverseProvider`, immutable `RawArtifactStore`, then `canonical.load_games.load_games_csv`; full feature ingestion is `ingest.ingest_nflverse` including per-season play-by-play. The six-model runner is `backtest.walkforward.run_walkforward(session, WalkForwardConfig(test_season=N))`. It must not be run against the original populated database for this release.

The new comparison reuses existing home-field, rolling and ratings models, distribution objects, scoring and replay functions. Record the genuine source artifact hash and capture time; explicit training/validation/test seasons; every attempted candidate; model/config/code hash; per-game predictions; all losses and uncertainty intervals. No closing data enter these predictors. Keep the full failed comparison, not just the best approach. Report `RESEARCH ONLY`, `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`, `VALIDATION FAILED` and `ELIGIBLE FOR FURTHER REVIEW` distinctly; none authorizes recommendations. Retrospective success alone cannot satisfy the prospective gate.

## Player-prop experiment preparation

Start with **QB pass attempts**, then **RB carries** and **receiver targets/receptions**, as opportunity-count experiments. Historical GSIS-linked production and snaps can support these targets after a completeness audit; this is not yet a claim that complete historical training coverage is installed. Preserve roster assignment at the prediction cutoff, prior-game attempts/carries/targets, team dropbacks/rush rate, rest and lagged snap share. Separate DNP, inactive, missing record and zero opportunities; do not drop them as if interchangeable. Keep trades and role changes in the appropriate historical team context. A current roster is not a historical eligibility list.

Compare prior-game/rolling role baselines against Poisson and overdispersed count models, including a participation hurdle when warranted. Fit every transformation and role classification on earlier games. Report count log score/deviance, MAE, quantile coverage and calibration at held-out thresholds by position, season and usage tier. Passing/rushing/receiving yards require an additional opportunity-to-yardage distribution and are a later experiment. Receptions require conditional catch rates; touchdown probabilities need rare-event calibration and sufficient trials. None supplies an actionable prop without an exact source-timestamped offered line and price. Snap share is not route participation, and missing routes must remain missing.

## Future SGP joint-probability plan

1. Establish exact sportsbook settlement definitions, permitted leg sets, alternate thresholds, push/void handling and authorized combined prices before value claims.
2. Fit a game-level joint generative structure: pace/plays and score state, team pass/rush decisions, personnel participation, player opportunity allocation, catches/yardage/TD outcomes. Enforce conservation and mutually exclusive outcome constraints rather than independently sampling incompatible player totals.
3. Estimate dependence on chronological historical games, with shrinkage and uncertainty. Compare empirical frequency and conservative reference joint models; validate held-out combinations, conditional calibration, rare-event tails and sensitivity to inactive/role scenarios.
4. Store immutable model versions, input vintages, joint samples and exact leg contracts. Compare to the **actual** permitted bet365 Tennessee combined quote; independently multiplied marginal probabilities are never an acceptable substitute.
5. Keep NO BET when identity, availability completeness, combinability, source price freshness or joint validation is inadequate. Prospective frozen evaluation and independent review remain required before any promotion.
