It is not a licensed-feed certification or an assertion that these markets do not exist. It does not grant API access or establish that an aggregator's international/New Jersey feed matches Tennessee offers.

## Documentary contradictions requiring resolution

- OpticOdds' [FAQ](https://developer.opticodds.com/page/faq-page) describes different request limits than its newer SGP guide. Its [historical marketing page](https://opticodds.com/historical-odds) describes broader retention than the historical endpoint contract. Use written entitlement and measured responses, not the more favorable statement.
- SportsGameOdds' [rate guide](https://sportsgameodds.com/docs/info/rate-limiting) and pricing FAQ give different hourly object ceilings. Runtime `/account/usage` and the contract must govern. Its [AI article](https://sportsgameodds.com/blog/odds-api-for-ai-betting-apps) endorses modeling/storage while binding terms contain broad derivative restrictions. This is an unresolved licensing question, not permission inferred from advertising.
- Odds-API.io's NFL landing page still offers free signup; its current pricing page says new free keys are paused. We did not attempt signup or treat a money-back paid plan as free sample access.

## Actual sample validation

The documented credential-free `GET https://api.odds-api.io/v3/bookmakers` returned HTTP 200 on 2026-10-14. The retained [observation](evidence/release1b3/market-catalog-observation.json) contains capture time, SHA-256 of the UTF-8 response content, total count (429), and the exact two relevant catalog entries. A second fetch reproduced the same relevant entries. The artifact is a selected metadata response, not the entire catalog. `createdAt` is catalog creation time, **not an odds timestamp**.

Result: 0 market quotes and 0 SGP quotes verified; 0 TN confirmations. Credentialed endpoints were not probed, credentials were not searched for, and documentation examples were not ingested as real prices. Thus a representative market-sample acceptance remains blocked on authorized access. The public sample is useful jurisdiction evidence, not a successful feed test.

## Concrete decision request for the owner

Recommended decision: **do not purchase a feed yet**. Authorize a no-cost vendor feasibility inquiry or provide an existing evaluation key through the application's secure configuration, only after the vendor supplies these written answers. No inquiry has been sent.

1. Does the entitled feed explicitly represent bet365 **Tennessee**, and by which book/jurisdiction IDs? Are international and NJ prices ever substituted? What upstream access rights cover the supplied data?
2. May this private app persist immutable raw observations indefinitely, train/evaluate statistical models, display derived research, and retain/publish appropriately redacted validation evidence? What remains permitted after subscription ends?
3. Provide actual current NFL samples for two-sided ML/spread/total, alternates, passing/rushing/receiving/receptions and TD contracts, plus a mixed game/player SGP accepted by bet365 TN. Include book event/market/selection IDs, periods, OT/void/settlement rules, suspension state, source time, vendor time, jurisdiction and freshness guarantees.
4. For exact SGP: provide requested leg set, accepted leg set, rejection reasons, book quote ID, unboosted combined price, quote time/expiry and rules for line changes, inactive/void legs and account-specific offers. Confirm this is the sportsbook's offered combined price, not a proprietary fair-price engine or another book.
5. Confirm trial scope without billing activation, eventual total monthly cost, quotas, history depth, rate limits, and whether historical data preserves original publication/observation vintages.

First investigate OpticOdds and SportsGameOdds for TN singles; their public evidence does not justify assuming either can satisfy SGP. Odds-API.io requires explicit TN clarification before a trial has value. A sample failing jurisdiction or rights checks should be rejected before engineering an adapter.

## Acceptance contract after authorized access

Use an isolated research workspace and preserve requests without credentials, raw responses where licensed, payload hashes, receipt times and provider timestamps separately. Join canonical game/player IDs through explicit reviewed crosswalks; reject ambiguous mappings. Check both sides and exact lines, all pagination/entitlement notices, periods, settlement rules and missing/suspended offers. Repeated observations must be idempotent; revised offers append evidence. Do not backdate historical capture to the provider timestamp.

Compare a permitted representative sample against the owner's contemporaneous Tennessee offer, including a changed line and rejected SGP combination. Measure quote age and latency across multiple captures, not a single advertised refresh figure. A response with no book-origin timestamp must retain that uncertainty. A per-book `available` flag cannot prove a specific account can place the wager.

Preserve **NO BET** for absent jurisdiction proof, incomplete/stale prices, unsupported settlements, unvalidated models or unavailable combined SGP quotes. Neither consensus odds nor a synthetic parlay price may fill those gaps.

## Future player-prop and SGP prerequisites

Passing yards/attempts/completions, rushing yards/attempts, receiving yards/receptions and passing TDs are plausible research targets only after outcome completeness, canonical identities and pregame role/availability vintages are demonstrated across seasons. Receiving targets and snaps can describe opportunity; snaps are not routes. TD markets additionally need defensible rare-event, role and scoring models. No provider market catalog makes any model validated.

The future joint engine should simulate shared game pace, score state, team opportunities, player participation/role and correlated production, then settle each exact leg from the same simulated game. Handle discrete pushes, ties, DNP/void rules, overtime, mutually exclusive legs and shared-player dependencies. Validate marginals and joint events on untouched chronological data with explicit sample sizes, calibration and uncertainty, including injury/role regimes. Independence products are only a diagnostic baseline, never accepted SGP probability. A validated joint estimate still needs the exact eligible bet365 TN combined offer before value can be assessed.
